# Validation Decision History

Append-only, dated record of Validation Decisions — institutional
decisions arising from validation evidence. Every decision cites a
Validation Theme (which cites its observations), names the deciding
authority (standing implementation authority or Founder Office, per
the [Operational Standard](founder-validation-program.md) §8), and
links into the institution's evolution record: the authorization,
blueprint, commits, and as-built records that carried any resulting
change. Entries follow the
[decision template](templates/validation-decision.md); nothing is
rewritten, later reversals are new dated entries citing the old.

Identifier convention: `FVD-<seq>` (program-scoped).
