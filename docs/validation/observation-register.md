# Founder Validation Register

Append-only registry of Founder Validation observations. One line per
observation; the observation file under `observations/` is the full
record. Lines are added at recording and amended only to reflect the
record's own dated amendments (theme assignment, evidence strength
restatement, disposition, closure). Nothing is removed; rejected
observations remain listed, marked by their disposition.

Identifier convention: `FVO-<cycle>-<seq>` per the
[Operational Standard](founder-validation-program.md) §2.
